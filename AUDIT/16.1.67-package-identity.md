- Continues the existing Stockland Ethiopia ERP 16.1.66 candidate lineage and carries its previously reviewed procurement/warehouse authority corrections forward.

The test suite includes the specified ETB 30,000 case (employee pension 2,100; PAYE base 27,900; PAYE 7,715; net pay 20,185).
